===============================================================
SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
E-Commerce Platform Services
Effective Date: August 1, 2024
===============================================================

1. SERVICE LEVEL TARGETS
===============================================================

1.1 UPTIME COMMITMENT
- Target Uptime: 99.5% per calendar month
- Measurement Period: Monthly (1st to last day of month)
- Exclusions: Scheduled maintenance windows (max 4 hours/month), customer-caused outages

1.2 UPTIME BREACH TIERS
- 99.0% to 99.49%: 5% service credit
- 98.5% to 98.99%: 10% service credit
- 98.0% to 98.49%: 15% service credit
- Below 98.0%: 25% service credit + escalation

1.3 RESPONSE TIME (API)
- Target: 95th percentile ≤ 500ms
- Critical threshold: 99th percentile ≤ 2000ms
- Measurement: Every API request
- Breach: If 95th percentile exceeds 500ms for 5+ consecutive hours

1.4 ERROR RATE
- Target: < 0.1% (errors / total requests)
- Warning threshold: 0.05%
- Critical threshold: > 0.5%
- Breach: If error rate exceeds 0.1% for 30+ minutes

1.5 DATABASE AVAILABILITY
- Target: 99.9% availability
- Measurement: Heartbeat check every 5 minutes
- Breach: 3 consecutive failed health checks = 30-minute downtime incident

2. PENALTY CALCULATION
===============================================================

2.1 MONTHLY SERVICE CREDIT CALCULATION
Base: Monthly Service Fee = $50,000

Credit Calculation:
- Per 0.01% below target: $50 credit
- Example: 99.2% uptime = 0.3% below 99.5% = $1,500 credit

2.2 INCIDENT RESPONSE SLA
- P1 (Critical): Response within 15 minutes
- P2 (High): Response within 1 hour
- P3 (Medium): Response within 4 hours
- Breach: +$500 per missed SLA window

2.3 RESOLUTION TIME
- P1 incidents: Must resolve within 4 hours (escalation every 30 min)
- P2 incidents: Must resolve within 8 hours
- P3 incidents: Must resolve within 24 hours
- Breach: Additional $250 per hour over SLA

3. MEASUREMENT & REPORTING
===============================================================

3.1 METRICS COLLECTION
- Real-time monitoring via agents
- 5-minute granularity for all metrics
- Monthly reports due by 5th of following month

3.2 CREDITS
- Credits issued as account adjustments within 30 days of month-end
- Maximum monthly credit: 25% of monthly fee ($12,500)
- Credits cannot be carried forward beyond 12 months

4. EXCLUSIONS
===============================================================

- Third-party provider outages (AWS, CDN)
- Customer misuse or DDoS attacks
- Scheduled maintenance (4-hour/month window, announced 48 hours in advance)
- Customer network issues

5. CONTACT & ESCALATION
===============================================================

Support Level 1: support@platform.com (4-hour response)
Support Level 2: escalations@platform.com (1-hour response)
Executive Escalation: ceo@platform.com (15-minute response for P1)

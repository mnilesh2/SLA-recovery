SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

PARTIES:
Service Provider: TechCorp Solutions Inc.
Customer: Acme Corporation

1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION
TechCorp shall provide cloud hosting and API services to Acme Corporation as described in Schedule A.

2. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS

2.1 UPTIME COMMITMENT
Service Provider commits to maintain System availability of 99% (Four Nines) during all hours.
- Monthly Uptime Target: 99%
- Measurement Period: Calendar Month
- Excludes scheduled maintenance windows (notified 48 hours in advance)

2.2 RESPONSE TIME COMMITMENT
Service Provider commits to maintain maximum average response time of 2 seconds (2000ms).
- Measurement: Per transaction
- Target: 95th percentile response time shall not exceed 2 seconds

2.3 ERROR RATE COMMITMENT
Service Provider commits to maintain data accuracy of 99.9%.
- Measurement: Successful transactions / Total transactions
- Target: 99.9% accuracy rate

3. SERVICE CREDITS (PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE)

3.1 UPTIME PENALTIES
If monthly uptime falls below 99%, Service Provider shall pay customer the following monthly service credits:

- 99% - 98.9%: 5% of monthly service fee
- 98.9% - 98%: 10% of monthly service fee
- 98% - 95%: 15% of monthly service fee
- Below 95%: 25% of monthly service fee

3.2 RESPONSE TIME PENALTIES
For each incident where 95th percentile response time exceeds 2 seconds:
- Exceeds 2 seconds: 2% of monthly service fee per incident
- Exceeds 5 seconds: 5% of monthly service fee per incident
- Exceeds 10 seconds: 10% of monthly service fee per incident

3.3 CALCULATION OF PENALTIES
Penalties shall be calculated as follows:
- Monthly Service Fee: $10,000
- Penalty Per Incident: Penalty Percentage × Monthly Service Fee
- Maximum Monthly Credits: 30% of monthly service fee

4. MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

4.1 MEASUREMENT METHODOLOGY
Uptime, Response Time, and Error Rate shall be measured using TechCorp's monitoring tools.

4.2 INCIDENT REPORTING
Service Provider shall report incidents affecting SLA metrics within 24 hours.

4.3 MONTHLY REPORTS
Detailed SLA reports shall be provided by the 5th business day of each month.

5. EXCLUSIONS AND EXCEPTIONS

5.1 FORCE MAJEURE
SLA commitments do not apply to incidents caused by:
- Natural disasters
- Act of God
- War or terrorism
- Government actions
- Customer-caused failures

5.2 SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE
SLA credits do not apply to:
- Scheduled maintenance (with 48-hour notice)
- Emergency security patches (with 24-hour notice)

5.3 CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY
SLA commitments do not apply when:
- Customer exceeds agreed bandwidth limits
- Customer misconfigures systems
- Customer does not follow security guidelines

6. REMEDIES AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

6.1 EXCLUSIVE REMEDY
Service credits are the exclusive remedy for SLA breaches.

6.2 CLAIMS
Customer must submit SLA credit claims within 30 days of month end.

6.3 DISPUTE ESCALATION
Disputes shall be escalated to executive management within 10 business days.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION

This SLA is effective for the duration of the Service Agreement.
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.

8. AMENDMENT

This SLA may be amended with 60 days written notice by either party.
